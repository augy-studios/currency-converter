# Google Play release notes, 1.0.0

Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes** box in Play Console (GOOGLE-PLAY.md,
section 9). Play allows 500 characters per language; this is about 440. The tag must match a
language the store listing has: the app is in English only, so the listing's default language is
English (United Kingdom), `en-GB`. If Play shows the tag as one it doesn't know, change it to the
one Play lists.

```text
<en-GB>
The Currency Converter is now uwuCurrency, rebuilt as a real app.
• History: every conversion kept on your phone, tap one to convert it again
• Share your history with your other devices with a code, no account needed
• Rates over time for up to six currencies, and it works offline
• Material You by default, with the UwU Apps look a switch away in Theme
• Share a conversion as a picture, or share "50 USD" from any app to convert it
• Report a bug from the edge tray, with screenshots
</en-GB>
```

Play shows its own update notices, so the Google Play build has no update bar of its own; its
Updates page still lists every version's notes, with **Update on Google Play**.
