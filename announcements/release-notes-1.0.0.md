The Currency Converter is now uwuCurrency, an app: for Android, in Material You by default, and for Windows, macOS and Linux, with everything the website does and more, including a history of your conversions you can share between your devices.

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**. Have the Currency Converter from Google Play? It updates there by itself; the GitHub app and the Play app can't update each other, so keep to one.

**Windows**: run `uwucurrency-1.0.0-setup.exe`. It's not signed with a paid certificate, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: **More info** > **Run anyway**. It installs for you alone, no administrator needed.

**Mac**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuCurrency to Applications. The first time, right-click the app > **Open**. If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/uwuCurrency.app` in Terminal.

**Linux**: `sudo apt install ./uwucurrency-1.0.0-amd64.deb`, or `sudo dnf install ./uwucurrency-1.0.0-x86_64.rpm`. For the AppImage, make it executable and run it.

Your last conversion and theme come across from the website on the same device, and **Share with another device** copies your history between any of them.

## What's new

### Everywhere
- **History**: every conversion is kept on your device, newest first. Tap one to convert it again at today's rate; remove one, or clear them all.
- **Share with another device**: copy your history and theme straight to your other phone, tablet or computer with a code or a QR code, no account needed. It goes from device to device, never through a server.
- **Report a bug**: from the edge tray, with up to three pictures, including a screenshot of the whole page. You get the report's ID to quote.
- **Show me around**: a short tour the first time you open it, which scrolls to each thing it shows.
- **The edge tray**: Theme, History, Share, Show me around and Report a bug, in reach at the top right.

### Android app
- **A real app**: every screen built for Android, quick to open, and the last rates kept for when you're offline.
- **Material You by default**: your phone's font and, on Android 12 and later, your wallpaper's colours. The UwU Apps look you know from the website, with its seven colours and the Jua font, is one switch away in **Theme**.
- **Updates page**: what's new in this version and every one before it, and **Update** downloads the next version, checks it and installs it.
- **New versions of the app**: turn it on in the Updates page for a notification the moment a new version is out, even with the app closed.
- **Share a conversion**: as a picture and words through Android's share sheet, or straight to Telegram, WhatsApp, X or Facebook, or save it as a picture.
- **Prices shared from other apps**: share "50 USD" or "€12" to uwuCurrency and it's filled in, ready to convert.

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Glass, as your computer draws it**: besides the UwU Apps look, **Theme** offers Fluent 2 with Mica on Windows, macOS Tahoe's glass on a Mac, and Deepin and COSMIC's frosted glass on Linux, in your accent colour.
- **Make it yours**: the first window picks the theme, the colour (or your computer's own accent colour) and whether uwuCurrency starts when you sign in.
- **Its own title bar**, with a **command bar** (`Ctrl+K`): type "100 usd to eur" to convert it, search your history and every command, or paste a link.
- **The converter and the graph side by side**: on a wide window, the converter and your history sit on the left while the graph fills the rest.
- **Tray rate**: right-click a conversion in History and pick **Set as Tray Rate** to see that rate in the tray's tooltip (and beside the menu bar icon on a Mac).
- **Desktop widgets**: pin a currency against a few others to your desktop, in a small window you can keep on top.
- **Your pairs in the Jump List and the Dock**, and the taskbar's thumbnail buttons step through them.
- **Screenshots**, including a long one of the whole page, copied and saved to Downloads.
- **Right-click menus**, a pointing hand on everything you can press, keyboard shortcuts and, on a Mac, the menu bar.
- **Updates itself**: it checks as it opens and every hour, downloads the update and offers **Restart to update**.
