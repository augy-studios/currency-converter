✨NEW✨ uwuCurrency 1.0.0: the Currency Converter, now an app
currency.uwuapps.org

Everywhere:
- History: every conversion kept on your device, tap one to convert it again
- Share your history with your other devices with a code, no account needed
- Report a bug with a screenshot of the whole page, right from the edge tray

Android app:
- Every screen built for Android, and it works offline with the last rates
- Material You by default, with the UwU Apps look a switch away in Theme
- Share a conversion as a picture, or share "50 USD" from any app to convert it
- An Updates page, and a notification the moment a new version is out

Windows, macOS and Linux apps:
- Fluent 2, macOS Tahoe or frosted glass looks, in your accent colour
- A command bar (Ctrl+K): type "100 usd to eur" to convert it
- A rate in the tray, and rate widgets on your desktop
- Updates itself
